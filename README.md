# FUTURE_DS_01 – Business Sales Performance Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing business sales data to identify revenue trends, top-performing products, profitable categories, and regional sales performance. The dashboard provides actionable insights to support data-driven business decisions.

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze overall business sales performance
- Identify top-selling products and categories
- Compare sales across different regions
- Track important business KPIs
- Generate actionable business recommendations

## 🛠️ Tools Used
- Tableau Public

## 📊 Dashboard Features
- Total Sales KPI
- Total Profit KPI
- Total Orders KPI
- Sales by Category
- Sales by Region
- Top Performing Products
- Profit Analysis

## 📈 Key Insights
- Identified the highest revenue-generating products.
- Compared regional sales performance to identify strong and weak markets.
- Analyzed category-wise sales and profitability.
- Tracked business KPIs for performance evaluation.

## 💡 Business Recommendations
- Increase focus on high-performing products.
- Improve marketing strategies in low-performing regions.
- Optimize inventory for profitable product categories.
- Use sales insights to improve business growth and decision-making.
